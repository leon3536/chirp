# RGAS OPERATOR CARD — game audio in 3 steps

*(Print one page, laminate, leave at the scorekeeper bench. RGAS §7.4, §8.6.)*

## Start

1. **Open the laptop.**
2. **Double-click the desktop icon “RGAS Soundboard.”**
3. Wait for the soundboard page to open (about 10 seconds). Green dot
   top-left = ready.

## During the game

- **Press a button to play it.** Pressing another button switches to it.
- **GOAL HORN** is the big red button at the top. Press it the moment the
  goal is scored — it cuts off whatever is playing.
- **Warmups:** press **▶ Shuffle warmup** once; music runs on its own.
- **Big red STOP (bottom right)** silences everything, instantly.
  **FADE** (next to it) fades out over 2 seconds — nicer for music.

Wrong song? Press STOP, press the right one. You cannot break anything from
this screen.

## If something is wrong

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Page won’t load / closed the browser | Double-click the desktop icon again |
| Buttons work but **no sound in the rink** | Wait 10 seconds — it reconnects by itself. Still silent? Close the laptop lid, open it, double-click the icon again |
| Laptop died | Plug it in, power on, double-click the icon |
